## What Drives Water Damage Restoration Cost

**Water category matters enormously.** Category 1 is clean water — supply line break, overflow from a clean fixture. Category 2 is gray water — dishwasher overflow, washing machine discharge. Category 3 is black water — sewage backup, floodwater. Category 3 jobs require full contamination protocols: PPE, antimicrobial treatment, disposal of porous materials. The cost difference between a Category 1 and Category 3 job of the same size can be 50–100%.

**Time.** Water that sits for 48 hours in Limestone County's humid climate has often already started microbial growth. Mold remediation on top of water restoration adds $1,500–$6,000 to the bill. Every hour matters in the first 72 hours.

**Structural materials affected.** Wet drywall must be removed. Wet insulation must be removed. Wet subfloor may need to come out. Wet hardwood may cup beyond recovery. These are not cleaning costs — they're demolition and rebuild costs that fall under a different category of work.

**Square footage.** Extraction, drying equipment placement, monitoring, and documentation scale with the affected area.

## Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Water Damage in Athens?

Generally yes — for sudden and accidental water damage. A burst pipe at 2am, a refrigerator water line failure, a washing machine overflow. These are standard covered perils under most Alabama homeowners policies.

What's typically not covered: flooding from outside the home (requires flood insurance through NFIP), and gradual leaks — a slow drip under the sink that went unnoticed for months. The distinction matters, and it's where disputes with adjusters happen.
