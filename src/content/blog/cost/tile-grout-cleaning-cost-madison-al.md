## What Determines Tile Cleaning Prices in Madison?

### Square Footage

Most companies price per square foot. A typical Madison kitchen runs 150-300 sq ft. A master bathroom: 80-150 sq ft. A full home with tile in multiple rooms: 400-800 sq ft.

### Grout Width and Pattern

Smaller grout lines (1/8 inch) hold less dirt but are harder to reach. Larger grout lines (3/8 inch or wider) hold more grime and take longer. Herringbone and diagonal patterns create more grout line per square foot.

### Soil and Stain Level

Years of grease buildup in a kitchen requires longer dwell time and stronger solutions than a bathroom with surface soap scum. Heavy mildew in shower tile costs more to treat.

### Sealing

Sealing after cleaning protects grout from future staining. Most professionals recommend it. Adds $0.50-$1.00/sq ft. Worth every dollar on kitchen and bathroom grout.

## What Does Professional Tile Cleaning Include?

A proper professional tile cleaning job:

1. **Pre-inspection** — identify grout type (cement, epoxy, sanded, unsanded), tile material, and problem areas
2. **Pre-treatment** — alkaline solution applied to break down grease and soil
3. **Dwell time** — solution sits 5-10 minutes to penetrate grout pores
4. **High-pressure steam extraction** — removes loosened soil without damaging grout
5. **Rinse** — removes solution residue
6. **Dry** — tile dries within 30-60 minutes
7. **Sealing** (if included) — applied after tile is dry

What is NOT included unless quoted separately:
- Grout recoloring
- Caulk replacement in shower corners
- Tile repair or replacement
- Mold remediation (if mold has penetrated behind tile)

## Madison-Specific Tile Concerns

### Humidity and Mildew

Madison sits at 640 feet elevation in the Tennessee Valley. Summer humidity regularly exceeds 80%. Shower tile and grout in Madison homes grow mildew faster than drier climates. If you see black specks in grout — that is mildew, not just dirt.

### Hard Water Deposits

Madison water comes from the Tennessee Valley utility system. Hard water leaves calcium and mineral deposits on tile, especially around faucets and in showers. These require acid-based cleaners, not alkaline ones. A good tech identifies this before treatment.

### New Construction Grout Haze

Many newer Madison homes in developments like Signature Pointe have a film of grout haze left from installation. This is different from normal soil and requires a different treatment — usually a diluted acid wash. Not all companies know the difference.

## DIY vs. Professional Tile Cleaning

### What Mopping Does

Mopping spreads clean water across the tile surface. It does not penetrate grout pores, does not remove embedded soil, and does not kill mildew at the root. It keeps the surface looking acceptable, nothing more.

### What Store-Bought Grout Cleaners Do

The oxygen-based foaming products you see at hardware stores (OxiClean, Grout Shield) provide some improvement on lightly soiled grout. They are not effective on heavily soiled or mildewed grout. Bleach kills surface mildew but does not remove the organic matter feeding it — mildew returns faster.

### What Professional Cleaning Does

Professional truck-mounted steam extraction operates at 200+ degrees Fahrenheit at 1,200+ PSI. It reaches grout pores that surface cleaners cannot. The difference on before-and-after photos is typically dramatic — especially on grout that has been neglected for 3-5 years.

## Is Tile Cleaning Worth It in Madison?

Yes, for two reasons:

**Appearance:** Professionally cleaned and sealed grout looks like new tile. Realtors in Madison consistently report that clean tile and grout add perceived value when listing a home.

**Longevity:** Unsealed grout absorbs moisture, which leads to cracking and substrate damage. In a shower, that means water intrusion and eventually mold behind the wall. Sealing after a professional clean extends the life of your tile installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does tile cleaning take?**
A typical Madison kitchen and two bathrooms take 2-3 hours. Larger tile areas take longer.

**How long until I can walk on the tile?**
Tile is dry within 30-60 minutes. Sealed tile needs 2-4 hours to cure fully.

**How often should tile be professionally cleaned?**
Every 12-18 months for kitchen and bathroom tile. Every 24 months for low-traffic tile.

**Can grout be made white again?**
If the original color was white or light gray, professional cleaning often restores most of it. For full color restoration, grout recoloring applies a permanent pigmented sealant.
