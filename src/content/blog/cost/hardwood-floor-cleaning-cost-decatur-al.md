## Levels of Hardwood Floor Service

**Level 1 -- Basic Cleaning:** Dry dust mopping, professional pH-neutral solution, and buff-dry. Removes surface dirt. Good for maintenance every 6-12 months.

**Level 2 -- Deep Cleaning:** Professional cleaning solution, mechanical scrubbing with a buffer, and extraction of loosened soil. Removes embedded grime and residue from consumer products.

**Level 3 -- Buff-and-Coat:** Light abrasion of existing finish, new layer of polyurethane applied. Best for floors that have lost gloss but have no deep scratches. Lasts 3-5 years. Cost: $1.50-$3.00/sqft.

**Level 4 -- Full Sand and Refinish:** Sanding to bare wood, staining (optional), and multiple coats of new finish. Removes deep scratches and stains. Lasts 10-15 years. Cost: $3.00-$6.00/sqft.

## Decatur Hardwood Floor Considerations

### Tennessee River Humidity

Decatur river proximity means higher humidity year-round. Hardwood expands and contracts with seasonal humidity changes. Excess moisture from over-wet cleaning causes warping. Professional low-moisture cleaning avoids water intrusion into seams.

### Red Clay and Grit

Red clay soil and Tennessee River area grit are abrasive. They scratch hardwood finish when walked on. Regular professional cleaning removes these abrasives before they damage the finish.

### Steam Mops

Steam mops force moisture into wood seams, causing swelling and finish delamination. Avoid them on hardwood floors.

### Older Decatur Homes

Many older Decatur homes have hardwood with shellac, wax, or early polyurethane finishes requiring specialized cleaning methods.
