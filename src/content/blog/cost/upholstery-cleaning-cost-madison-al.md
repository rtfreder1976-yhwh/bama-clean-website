## What Determines Upholstery Cleaning Cost in Madison?

### Fabric Type

Each fabric requires a different cleaning method:

- **Microfiber:** Common in Madison homes. Requires careful moisture control to avoid water rings.
- **Cotton and linen:** Absorb stains deeply. Usually safe for water-based cleaning.
- **Velvet:** Pile must be brushed during cleaning to prevent matting. Higher cost.
- **Leather:** Cleaning and conditioning, not extraction. Runs $100-$300 for a sofa.
- **Silk and wool:** Dry-clean method only.

### Piece Size

A loveseat has roughly 40% of the surface area of a large sectional. Pricing scales with the amount of fabric that needs treatment.

### Soil Level

Light soil: standard pricing. Heavy pet odor, embedded food stains, or smoke: add $30-$60 per piece. Pet urine saturation requires enzyme treatment at $25-$50 extra.

## The Fabric Code System

Every upholstered piece has a cleaning code tag, usually under the seat cushion:

- **W** -- Water-based cleaning only
- **S** -- Solvent-based only
- **WS** -- Either method safe
- **X** -- Vacuum only

Using the wrong method ruins the fabric. An S-coded fabric treated with water causes permanent water rings. Professional cleaners read the code before touching the piece.

## Pet Households in Madison

Madison neighborhoods like Providence and Redstone Village are full of families with dogs and cats. Pet dander embeds in upholstery over time. Vacuuming removes surface hair but not the dander and oils in the fabric.

For pet households:
- Enzyme treatment breaks down the proteins that cause odor
- Hot water extraction removes loosened material
- If foam cushion fill is saturated with urine, replacement is needed -- no surface treatment reaches the interior

## What Professional Upholstery Cleaning Does Not Fix

- Permanent dye stains from bleach, wine, or chemicals
- Worn or pilling fabric
- Set-in ink or marker
- Deeply saturated pet urine in foam fill

A good technician will tell you upfront what is achievable before starting.

## Madison Considerations
