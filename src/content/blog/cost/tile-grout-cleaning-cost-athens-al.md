## What Makes Grout So Hard to Clean?

Grout is porous concrete. Every time you mop, you push dirty water into those pores. Over time, the grout locks in oil, soap residue, food particles, and mildew. By the time grout looks dark, it's been accumulating that buildup for years.

Consumer scrub brushes and bleach-based cleaners lighten the surface temporarily. They don't extract what's inside the pore. A week later it looks dark again — because it is dark again.

Professional tile cleaning uses truck-mounted high-pressure steam that blasts into the grout pore and extracts the soil. The difference is visible in real time. Athens homeowners who've watched us work describe it as watching years disappear off their floor.

## Should You Seal the Grout After Cleaning?

Yes — every time. Sealing after a professional cleaning extends the results by 3–5 years. Unsealed grout re-soils within months because the open pore acts like a sponge.
