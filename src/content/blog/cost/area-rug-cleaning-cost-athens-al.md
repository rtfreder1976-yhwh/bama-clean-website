## Why Rugs Cost More Than Wall-to-Wall Carpet

Wall-to-wall carpet gets cleaned in place. Area rugs need to be inspected for fiber type, dye stability, and construction before any chemistry touches them. The wrong solution on a wool rug causes dye bleed. Too much moisture on a natural fiber rug causes shrinkage. These aren't hypothetical risks — they happen every day when inexperienced cleaners treat an area rug like carpet.

BamaClean tests fiber and dye stability before cleaning. That step is non-negotiable. It's why we can clean a Persian or Turkish rug without turning it into an expensive problem.
