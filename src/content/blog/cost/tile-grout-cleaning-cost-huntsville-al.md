## What Determines Tile Cleaning Prices in Huntsville?

### Square Footage

A typical Huntsville kitchen runs 150-300 sq ft of tile. A master bathroom: 80-150 sq ft. A full home with tile in multiple rooms: 400-800 sq ft.

### Grout Width and Pattern

Smaller grout lines hold less dirt but are harder to reach. Larger grout lines hold more grime and take longer to clean.

### Soil and Stain Level

Years of grease buildup in a kitchen requires longer dwell time and stronger solutions than bathroom soap scum.

### Sealing

Sealing after cleaning protects grout from future staining. Worth every dollar on kitchen and bathroom grout.

## Huntsville-Specific Tile Concerns

### Hard Water Deposits

Huntsville water has high mineral content. Hard water leaves calcium and mineral deposits on tile, especially around faucets and in showers. These require acid-based cleaners, not alkaline ones. A professional identifies this before treatment.

### Humidity and Mildew

Huntsville summer humidity regularly exceeds 80%. Shower tile and grout grow mildew faster in humid climates. Black specks in grout are typically mildew, not just dirt.

### Research Park Area Homes

Many Huntsville homes near Research Park and Cummings Research Park were built in the 1960s-1990s with older tile and grout formulations. Older grout is more porous and absorbs staining more readily than modern grouts.

## Is Tile Cleaning Worth It in Huntsville?

Yes, for two reasons:

**Appearance:** Professionally cleaned and sealed grout looks like new tile. Before-and-after photos are consistently dramatic on neglected grout.

**Longevity:** Unsealed grout absorbs moisture, which leads to cracking and substrate damage. In a shower, that means water intrusion and eventually mold behind the wall.
