## What Drives Fire Restoration Costs in Huntsville?

### Fire Severity and Burn Area

Surface charring versus burned-through structural members is the difference between a $10,000 and a $100,000 job. A fire that stays contained to one room without structural involvement is far less expensive than one that compromises framing.

### Smoke Spread

Smoke travels fast through HVAC systems and pressure differentials. A fire in one room can fill an entire Huntsville home with smoke within minutes. Whole-home smoke contamination adds $10,000-$30,000 in labor and materials compared to a contained smoke area.

### Soot Type

- **Dry soot** (fast-burning fires, paper, wood): Powdery, easier to clean from hard surfaces
- **Wet soot** (slow-burning, smoldering fires, plastics): Sticky and difficult to remove
- **Protein residue** (kitchen fires, food burning): Nearly invisible but extremely pungent and bonds to surfaces

### Water Damage from Firefighting

Most significant fires also involve water damage from firefighting, adding extraction, drying, and mold prevention to the scope.

## The Fire Restoration Process

1. **Emergency board-up and tarping** -- secure the structure: $500-$2,500
2. **Safety assessment** -- structural inspection before interior work
3. **Contents inventory** -- photograph and document everything
4. **Water extraction** (if present)
5. **Smoke and soot removal** -- HEPA vacuuming, chemical sponge cleaning
6. **HVAC cleaning** -- essential if smoke entered ductwork
7. **Odor treatment** -- ozone generation, hydroxyl generation, thermal fogging
8. **Demo and rebuild** -- remove damaged materials and restore

## Does Insurance Cover Fire and Smoke Damage in Huntsville?

Yes. Fire and smoke damage is covered under standard homeowners insurance regardless of cause. Coverage includes structure, attached structures, personal property, and additional living expenses (hotel and meals while home is uninhabitable).

What to do immediately after a fire:
- Call 911 and wait for fire department clearance
- Call your insurance company within 24 hours
- Call a restoration company for emergency board-up and stabilization
- Document everything with photos before anything is moved

## Huntsville-Specific Considerations

### Older Home Wiring

Many older Huntsville homes near Twickenham and Five Points have electrical systems from the 1950s-1970s. Older wiring is a more common fire cause in these neighborhoods. After any fire involving electrical components, full electrical inspection is required before restoration.

### HVAC Systems

Most Huntsville homes have central HVAC. Running the HVAC after a fire spreads smoke throughout every room. HVAC cleaning after fire or smoke damage is essential -- not optional.

### Humidity and Secondary Damage

Huntsville humidity combined with firefighting water creates mold risk within 48 hours. Restoration must address both fire and smoke damage and water damage simultaneously.
