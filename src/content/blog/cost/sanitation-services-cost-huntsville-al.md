## What Determines Sanitation Service Costs in Huntsville?

### Square Footage

A standard 1,800 sq ft Huntsville home costs $150-$350 for full residential disinfection treatment.

### Treatment Method

- **Electrostatic spraying:** EPA-registered solution applied with electrostatic technology that wraps around surfaces for complete coverage. Dries in 10-15 minutes.
- **Thermal fogging:** Disinfectant converted to fog that penetrates HVAC, wall cavities, and hard-to-reach areas.
- **Ozone generation:** High-concentration ozone gas eliminates pathogens, bacteria, and odors. Requires vacating the property for 2-4 hours.

### Biohazard Level

Standard residential disinfection: lower cost. Biohazard events (sewage backup, trauma, hoarding situations) require full PPE and specialized disposal: $1,000-$10,000+.

## When Do You Need Professional Sanitation in Huntsville?

### Post-Illness

After a household illness involving norovirus, influenza, or C. difficile, standard surface cleaning is insufficient. EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants at proper concentration are required.

### Sewage Backup

Sewage contains fecal coliform, E. coli, and hepatitis A. After a sewage backup in a Huntsville home, professional sanitation is a health and safety requirement -- and typically required for insurance compliance.

### After Flooding

After water damage restoration drying, professional sanitation of affected surfaces is recommended before rebuilding.

### Schools and Commercial

Huntsville is home to numerous schools, daycares, medical offices, and research facilities. Professional disinfection services for commercial properties -- monthly or quarterly -- maintain baseline pathogen control.

### Real Estate Preparation

Professional sanitation before listing a Huntsville home eliminates odors and provides documented treatment that can be disclosed to buyers.

## Huntsville-Specific Considerations

Huntsville summer humidity exceeds 80%. High humidity supports faster bacterial and mold growth. Homes and commercial properties benefit from more frequent sanitation during summer months.

Huntsville City Schools and numerous daycares throughout the metro deal with illness outbreaks during the school year. Professional sanitation services reduce transmission between outbreaks.
