## What Drives Fire Restoration Costs in Decatur?

**Fire severity and burn area:** Surface charring versus burned-through structural members is the difference between a $10,000 and a $100,000 job.

**Smoke spread:** Smoke travels fast through HVAC systems. Whole-home smoke contamination adds $10,000-$30,000 compared to a contained smoke area.

**Soot type:** Dry soot (fast-burning fires) is easier to clean. Wet soot (smoldering fires, plastics) is sticky and difficult to remove. Protein residue (kitchen fires) is nearly invisible but extremely pungent.

**Water damage from firefighting:** Most significant fires involve water damage, adding extraction, drying, and mold prevention to the scope.

## The Fire Restoration Process

1. **Emergency board-up and tarping** -- secure the structure: $500-$2,500
2. **Safety assessment** -- structural inspection before interior work
3. **Contents inventory** -- document and photograph everything
4. **Water extraction** (if present)
5. **Smoke and soot removal** -- HEPA vacuuming, chemical sponge cleaning
6. **HVAC cleaning** -- essential after any fire with smoke
7. **Odor treatment** -- ozone generation, hydroxyl generation, thermal fogging
8. **Demo and rebuild** -- remove damaged materials and restore

## Does Insurance Cover Fire and Smoke Damage in Decatur?

Yes. Fire and smoke damage is covered by standard homeowners insurance regardless of cause. Coverage includes structure, attached structures, personal property, and additional living expenses (hotel and meals while home is uninhabitable).

Immediately after a fire:
- Call 911 and wait for fire department clearance
- Call your insurance company within 24 hours
- Call a restoration company for emergency board-up and stabilization
- Document everything with photos before anything is moved

## Decatur-Specific Considerations

### Humidity and Secondary Damage

Decatur Tennessee River humidity combined with firefighting water creates mold risk within 48 hours. Restoration must address both fire and smoke damage and water damage simultaneously.

### HVAC Systems

Running the HVAC after a fire spreads smoke throughout every room. HVAC cleaning after fire or smoke damage is essential -- not optional.

### Older Home Wiring

Some older Decatur neighborhoods have electrical systems from the 1950s-1970s. After any electrical fire, full electrical inspection is required before restoration.
