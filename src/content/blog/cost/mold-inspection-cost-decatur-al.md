## What Drives Mold Costs in Decatur?

**Scope of inspection:** Visual inspection identifies visible mold and moisture sources. Air sampling tells you mold type and spore concentration compared to outdoor baseline.

**Material type:** Mold on non-porous surfaces can be cleaned. Mold in porous materials (drywall, insulation, wood framing) must be removed and replaced.

**Containment:** Professional remediation requires poly barriers, negative air pressure, and air scrubbers -- adding $500-$2,000 to job cost.

## Decatur-Specific Mold Risks

### Tennessee River Humidity

Decatur river humidity creates faster mold growth than inland Alabama cities. Mold can begin growing within 24 hours of water intrusion. Speed of response matters more in Decatur than in drier areas.

### Flood-Prone Areas

Homes in Decatur flood zones that experience outside flooding are at extreme mold risk. Floodwater is Category 3 contaminated -- all porous materials that contact it must be removed. Any delay in starting remediation exponentially increases mold growth.

### Crawl Space Foundations

Many older Decatur homes have crawl space foundations. North Alabama crawl spaces accumulate moisture from ground evaporation. Crawl space encapsulation and remediation costs $2,000-$8,000 in Decatur.

### HVAC Systems

HVAC systems in Decatur accumulate condensation faster than in drier climates. Mold in ductwork distributes spores throughout the home every time the system runs.

## When Do You Need Testing vs. Remediation?

**Need testing if:**
- Musty odor without visible mold
- Unexplained respiratory symptoms in household members
- Water damage that may not have dried completely
- Buying or selling a home

**Skip to remediation if:**
- Visible mold covering more than 10 square feet
- Mold visible in crawl space or attic
