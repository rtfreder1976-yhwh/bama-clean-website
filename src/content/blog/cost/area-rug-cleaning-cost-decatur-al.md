## What Determines Area Rug Cleaning Cost?

**Fiber type:** Synthetic rugs cost least. Wool requires gentler solutions and longer dry times. Silk requires dry cleaning only. Jute and sisal cannot get wet.

**Construction:** Machine-made rugs: standard in-home cleaning. Hand-knotted rugs (Persian, Turkish, Afghan): require facility cleaning with full submersion and hand-washing.

**Soil level:** Light soil: standard pricing. Pet urine saturation requires enzyme treatment.

## In-Home vs. Facility Cleaning

**In-home:** Best for machine-made rugs. Effective for surface soil and standard maintenance.

**Facility:** Best for hand-knotted, silk, antique, or high-value rugs. Fully submerged, scrubbed from both sides, rinsed, and dried in a controlled environment. Pickup and delivery from Decatur adds $20-$50.

## Decatur-Specific Concerns

Tennessee River humidity creates mold risk for rugs on solid floors. Moisture trapped under rugs fosters mold growth on the backing. Signs: musty smell, discoloration on the underside. Professional cleaning followed by thorough drying prevents this.

Decatur pollen season is intense March through May. Rugs trap pollen and dust mites. Annual professional cleaning is recommended for allergy households.
