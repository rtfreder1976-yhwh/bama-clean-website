## Water Damage Category: The Biggest Cost Driver

### Category 1: Clean Water

Source: burst supply lines, overflow from sinks or tubs, rain entering from roof damage, appliance malfunctions with clean water.

**Typical cost:** $1,500-$5,000 for a single room. $3,000-$8,000 for multi-room incidents.

### Category 2: Gray Water

Source: dishwasher overflow, washing machine discharge, toilet overflow without feces, sump pump failure.

Contains biological or chemical contaminants. Affected materials may need replacement rather than drying.

**Typical cost:** $3,000-$8,000 for a single room. $6,000-$15,000 for multi-room incidents.

### Category 3: Black Water

Source: sewage backup, flooding from outside, standing water sitting for 72+ hours.

All porous materials that contacted Category 3 water must be removed. This is a full demo-and-rebuild scenario.

**Typical cost:** $5,000-$15,000+ for contained areas. $15,000-$50,000+ for whole-home events.

## Why Madison Water Damage Costs What It Does

### The 48-Hour Mold Clock

In Madison, with summer humidity regularly above 80%, mold begins growing in wet building materials within 24-48 hours. A Category 1 water event dried within 24 hours: $2,000-$4,000. The same event not addressed for 72 hours becomes a combined water and mold remediation: $6,000-$12,000.

Speed matters more in Madison than in drier climates.

### Madison Soil and Drainage

New developments in Madison -- Signature Pointe, Redstone Village, areas near Winchester Road -- are built on clay-heavy Tennessee Valley soil. Clay does not drain quickly. When storm drains overflow or foundations get overwhelmed, water intrusion can be significant.

### Does Insurance Cover Water Damage in Madison?

Standard homeowners insurance covers:
- Burst pipes (sudden and accidental)
- Appliance malfunctions
- Roof damage allowing rain in

Standard insurance does NOT cover:
- Gradual leaks (considered maintenance neglect)
- Ground flooding (requires separate flood insurance)
- Sewer backup (requires endorsement)

## The Water Damage Restoration Process

1. **Emergency extraction** -- remove standing water immediately
2. **Assessment** -- moisture mapping, category determination, photo documentation for insurance
3. **Drying equipment** -- industrial air movers and dehumidifiers placed strategically
4. **Monitoring** -- daily moisture readings until materials reach target levels
5. **Material removal** -- drywall, insulation, carpet/pad as needed
6. **Antimicrobial treatment** -- prevent mold in affected areas
7. **Documentation** -- drying logs for insurance claim
8. **Rebuild** -- framing, drywall, flooring, paint

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What should I do first when I discover water damage?**
Stop the water source if possible. Call a restoration company immediately. Do not use household fans -- they spread moisture rather than dry efficiently.

**How long does water damage restoration take in Madison?**
Extraction and drying: 5-7 days. Rebuild adds 2-8 weeks depending on scope.

**Should I call the restoration company or insurance first?**
