## What Determines Hardwood Cleaning Cost in Madison?

### Square Footage

Most companies price per square foot. A typical Madison home has 600-1,200 sq ft of hardwood. Larger open-plan homes in newer developments can have 1,500-2,000+ sq ft.

### Condition of the Finish

Hardwood in good shape with a solid polyurethane finish: standard cleaning cost. Worn, scratched, or dull finish: may need buff-and-coat or refinishing rather than cleaning alone.

### Type of Finish

- **Polyurethane (oil or water-based):** Most common in Madison homes. Durable, handles cleaning well.
- **Wax finish:** Older homes sometimes have waxed floors. Cannot be cleaned with water-based solutions -- requires special care.
- **Oil finish (Rubio, Bona Craft Oil):** Growing in newer builds. Requires oil-based maintenance products, not standard floor cleaner.
- **Aluminum oxide (prefinished hardwood):** Very durable. Common in newer Madison construction. Handles standard cleaning without issue.

### Furniture Moving

Most companies charge $10-$30 per heavy piece to move furniture. Move it yourself to save money.

## Levels of Hardwood Floor Service

### Level 1: Basic Cleaning

Dry dust mopping, professional pH-neutral solution application, and buff-dry. Removes surface dirt, dust, and light grime. Does not address scratches, scuffs, or finish dullness. Best for maintenance every 6-12 months.

**Cost:** $0.50-$0.80/sqft

### Level 2: Deep Cleaning

Professional cleaning solution, mechanical scrubbing with a low-RPM buffer, and extraction of loosened soil. Removes embedded grime, pet oils, and residue from consumer cleaning products. Restores appearance without touching the finish.

**Cost:** $0.75-$1.25/sqft

### Level 3: Buff-and-Coat (Screen-and-Recoat)

Light abrasion of the existing finish with a screen, followed by application of a new layer of polyurethane. Does NOT sand down to bare wood. Adds a fresh protective layer, fills minor surface scratches, and restores shine.

Best for: floors that have lost their gloss but have no deep scratches or damage.

**Cost:** $1.50-$3.00/sqft. Result lasts 3-5 years.

### Level 4: Full Sand and Refinish

Sanding down to bare wood, staining (optional), and applying multiple coats of new finish. Removes deep scratches, gouges, stains in the wood itself, and uneven wear. The most complete restoration.

**Cost:** $3.00-$6.00/sqft. Result lasts 10-15 years.

## Hardwood Floor Cleaning in Madison: Specific Considerations

### Humidity and Gapping

Madison sits in the Tennessee Valley with high summer humidity. Hardwood expands in summer and contracts in winter, creating seasonal gaps. These are normal and close when humidity rises again. Excessive gapping year-round may indicate an HVAC humidity problem.

Hardwood cleaning should use minimal moisture. Steam cleaners and wet mops damage hardwood over time by forcing water into the seams. Professional low-moisture cleaning avoids this.

### Alabama Red Clay and Pollen

Madison has two major floor-damage culprits: red clay soil tracked in from outside and heavy spring pollen deposits. Clay contains abrasive particles that scratch hardwood finish when walked on. Regular professional cleaning removes these abrasives before they damage the finish.

### Pet Damage

Pet nails scratch hardwood finish faster than foot traffic. Pet urine can stain and warp wood boards if not cleaned immediately. If boards have been warped or stained by pet urine, they typically need replacement -- cleaning cannot fix structural wood damage.

## DIY Hardwood Cleaning vs. Professional

**Consumer products:** Most store-bought hardwood cleaners (Murphy Oil Soap, Bona Consumer, Pledge) leave a residue that builds up over time and dulls the finish. They are also pH-incorrect for most finishes.

**Steam mops:** Marketed as safe for hardwood. They are not. Steam forces moisture into wood seams, causing swelling, warping, and finish delamination. Multiple manufacturers void warranties when steam mops are used.

**Professional cleaning:** pH-neutral solutions matched to finish type, controlled moisture application, and professional buffing equipment that does not leave residue or damage the finish.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does hardwood floor cleaning take?**
Most Madison homes take 1-3 hours for basic or deep cleaning. Buff-and-coat takes 3-4 hours plus dry time.

**How long until I can walk on the floors?**
Basic cleaning: 30-60 minutes. Buff-and-coat: 4-8 hours minimum. Full refinish: 24-48 hours.

**Can pet stains be removed from hardwood?**
Surface stains respond to enzyme cleaning. Stains that have penetrated the wood require sanding to remove.

**How often should hardwood be professionally cleaned?**
Deep cleaning every 12-18 months. Buff-and-coat every 3-5 years depending on traffic.
