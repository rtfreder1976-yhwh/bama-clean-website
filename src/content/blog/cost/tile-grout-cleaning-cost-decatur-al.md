## Decatur-Specific Tile Concerns

### Tennessee River Hard Water

Decatur water has high mineral content from the Tennessee River system. Hard water leaves calcium and mineral deposits on tile and around fixtures. These require acid-based cleaners, not alkaline ones.

### Humidity and Mildew

Decatur river humidity means shower tile and grout grow mildew faster than drier climates. Black specks in grout are typically mildew.

### Industrial Area Homes

Homes in Decatur near the industrial east side may accumulate more particulate on surfaces. Professional cleaning removes what general cleaning misses.
