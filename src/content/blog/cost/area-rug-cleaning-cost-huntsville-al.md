## What Determines Area Rug Cleaning Cost?

### Fiber Type

- **Synthetic (polypropylene, nylon, polyester):** Most affordable to clean. Standard methods work well.
- **Wool:** Natural fiber requiring gentler solutions and longer dry times. Very durable.
- **Cotton:** Absorbent and prone to color bleeding. Requires careful handling.
- **Silk:** Most expensive to clean. Specialty dry cleaning required.
- **Jute and sisal:** Cannot get wet without damage. Dry cleaning only.

### Construction Type

- **Machine-made (tufted or woven):** Standard cleaning. Lower cost.
- **Hand-knotted:** Persian, Turkish, Afghan, Indian rugs. Require facility cleaning with full submersion and hand-washing. Higher cost but appropriate for rugs worth $500-$10,000+.
- **Flatweave (kilim):** Usually cleaned in-facility. Medium cost.

### In-Home vs. Facility Cleaning

**In-home:** Best for machine-made rugs. The rug is cleaned in place using extraction equipment.

**Facility:** Best for hand-knotted, silk, antique, or high-value rugs. Fully submerged, scrubbed from both sides, rinsed thoroughly, and dried in a controlled environment. Pickup and delivery from Huntsville adds $20-$50.

## Huntsville-Specific Area Rug Concerns

### Pollen and Dust Mites

Huntsville ranks among the highest-pollen areas in the Southeast. Rugs trap pollen and dust mites -- both significant allergens. Annual professional cleaning is recommended for households with allergy sufferers.

### Humidity and Mold Risk

Summer humidity creates risk for rugs on solid floors. Moisture trapped under the rug fosters mold growth on the backing. Signs: musty smell, discoloration on the underside. Professional cleaning followed by thorough drying prevents this.

### Historic Home Rugs

Huntsville has numerous homes in the Twickenham Historic District and Five Points area. Antique and heirloom rugs in these homes require facility cleaning -- never in-home extraction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does area rug cleaning take?**
In-home cleaning: 30-90 minutes per rug. Facility cleaning: 3-7 days including pickup, cleaning, drying, and delivery.

**How long until the rug is dry?**
4-12 hours for most rugs with in-home cleaning. Hanging the rug speeds drying.

**How often should area rugs be professionally cleaned?**
High-traffic rugs: every 12-18 months. Low-traffic rugs: every 2-3 years. Pet households: every 6-12 months.
