## Why Hardwood Needs Professional Cleaning

Hardwood floors are deceptive. They look clean after mopping, but mopping pushes dirty water into the grain and the gaps between boards. Over time that builds up — dulling the finish, embedding pet dander, and creating the grimy film that makes floors look old before their time.

Consumer steam mops are the most common mistake. Too much moisture damages wood. It raises the grain, causes cupping, and can void finish warranties. A professional low-moisture cleaning system applies the right amount of solution, agitates it into the surface, and extracts it before it can penetrate into the wood.

## Athens-Specific Considerations

Limestone County's humidity affects hardwood floors more than most homeowners realize. Seasonal moisture swings cause hardwood to expand and contract. When soil builds up in the gaps between boards, it interferes with that natural movement and can accelerate joint wear.

Athens homes near the Elk River or in low-lying subdivisions sometimes see elevated indoor humidity that makes hardwood maintenance more important, not less. Regular professional cleaning removes the embedded soil before it becomes a structural issue.
