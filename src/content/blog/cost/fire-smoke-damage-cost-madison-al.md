## What Drives Fire Restoration Costs in Madison?

### Fire Severity and Burn Area

Structural fire damage costs are driven by what burned and how much. Surface charring versus burned-through structural members is the difference between a $10,000 and a $100,000 job.

### Smoke Spread

Smoke particles travel fast and far. A fire in one room can fill an entire home through HVAC systems, door gaps, and air pressure differentials. Smoke particles bond to every porous surface: drywall, insulation, wood, fabric, clothing.

The cost difference between a contained smoke area and whole-home smoke contamination is $10,000-$30,000 in labor and materials.

### Soot Type

Different fires produce different types of soot:

- **Dry soot** (fast-burning fires, paper, wood): Powdery, easier to clean from hard surfaces
- **Wet soot** (slow-burning, smoldering fires, plastics): Sticky and difficult to remove
- **Protein residue** (kitchen fires, food burning): Nearly invisible but extremely pungent. Bonds to surfaces.

### Water Damage from Firefighting

Most significant fires also involve water damage from firefighting. This adds extraction, drying, and potential mold prevention to the scope.

## The Fire Restoration Process

1. **Emergency board-up and tarping** -- secure the structure immediately. Cost: $500-$2,500
2. **Safety assessment** -- structural inspection before any interior work
3. **Contents inventory** -- photograph and document everything before moving
4. **Water extraction** (if present) -- remove firefighting water
5. **Smoke and soot removal** -- HEPA vacuuming, chemical sponge cleaning, wash-down of surfaces
6. **HVAC cleaning** -- smoke in ductwork must be cleaned or odor returns
7. **Odor treatment** -- ozone generation, hydroxyl generation, thermal fogging
8. **Demo** -- remove all char-damaged and unsalvageable materials
9. **Rebuild** -- framing, drywall, flooring, paint, fixtures

## Does Insurance Cover Fire and Smoke Damage in Madison?

Yes. Fire and smoke damage is covered under standard homeowners insurance regardless of how the fire started. Coverage includes:

- Structure (the home)
- Attached structures (garage)
- Personal property (contents)
- Additional living expenses (hotel and meals while home is uninhabitable)

What to do immediately:
- Call 911 and let fire department clear the scene
- Call your insurance company within 24 hours
- Call a restoration company for emergency board-up and stabilization
- Document everything with photos before anything is moved or cleaned

## Madison-Specific Considerations

### HVAC Systems

Most Madison homes have central HVAC. Smoke enters ductwork during a fire and deposits soot throughout the system. Running the HVAC after a fire spreads smoke particles to every room. Professional duct cleaning after fire and smoke damage is essential.

### Humidity and Secondary Damage

Water from firefighting combined with Madison humidity creates mold risk within 48 hours. Restoration must address both fire and smoke damage and water damage simultaneously.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can smoke-damaged contents be cleaned and saved?**
Many contents can be cleaned with professional methods. Electronics, documents, and soft goods are assessed individually.

**How long does fire restoration take?**
Emergency response: same day. Full restoration: 2-8 weeks for contained fires, 3-12 months for major structure fires.

**Can I stay in my home during restoration?**
A kitchen fire may allow the rest of the home to remain livable. Whole-home smoke or major structure damage usually requires temporary relocation.

**What should I NOT do after a fire?**
Do not run the HVAC. Do not clean soot yourself as it spreads. Do not throw away contents before insurance documents them.
