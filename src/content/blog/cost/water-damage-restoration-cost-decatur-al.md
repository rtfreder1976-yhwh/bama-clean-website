## Water Damage Category: The Biggest Cost Driver

### Category 1: Clean Water

Source: burst supply lines, overflow from sinks or tubs, rain entering from roof damage.

**Typical cost:** $1,500-$5,000 for a single room. $3,000-$8,000 for multi-room incidents.

### Category 2: Gray Water

Source: dishwasher overflow, washing machine discharge, toilet overflow without feces, sump pump failure.

**Typical cost:** $3,000-$8,000 for a single room. $6,000-$15,000 for multi-room incidents.

### Category 3: Black Water

Source: sewage backup, flooding from outside, standing water sitting 72+ hours.

**Typical cost:** $5,000-$15,000+ for contained areas. $15,000-$50,000+ for whole-home events.

## Decatur-Specific Water Damage Concerns

### Tennessee River Flooding

Decatur has FEMA-designated flood zones along the Tennessee River waterfront and surrounding creek systems. Standard homeowners insurance does not cover outside flooding -- this requires a separate National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policy.

If you live in a Decatur flood zone without flood insurance, outside water events are entirely your cost.

### Tennessee River Humidity and Mold

Decatur river humidity means mold begins growing in wet building materials within 24-48 hours -- potentially faster than most other Alabama cities. A Category 1 water event dried within 24 hours: $2,000-$4,000. The same event left 72 hours becomes combined water and mold remediation: $6,000-$12,000.

### Older Decatur Construction

Many older Decatur neighborhoods have homes from the 1950s-1970s with older insulation, plaster walls, and crawl space foundations. These materials are harder to dry and often need full replacement, increasing restoration costs.

## Does Insurance Cover Water Damage in Decatur?

Standard homeowners insurance covers:
- Burst pipes (sudden and accidental)
- Appliance malfunctions
- Roof damage allowing rain in

Standard insurance does NOT cover:
- Gradual leaks (maintenance neglect)
- Tennessee River flooding or storm surge (requires separate flood insurance)
- Sewer backup (requires endorsement)

## The Water Damage Restoration Process

1. **Emergency extraction** -- remove standing water immediately
2. **Assessment** -- moisture mapping, category determination, photo documentation
3. **Drying equipment** -- industrial air movers and dehumidifiers
4. **Monitoring** -- daily moisture readings
5. **Material removal** -- drywall, insulation, carpet/pad as needed
6. **Antimicrobial treatment** -- prevent mold
7. **Documentation** -- drying logs for insurance
8. **Rebuild** -- framing, drywall, flooring, paint
