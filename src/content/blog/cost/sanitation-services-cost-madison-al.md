## What Determines Sanitation Service Costs in Madison?

### Square Footage

Most services are priced per square foot or per room. A standard 1,800 sq ft Madison home costs $150-$350 for full residential disinfection.

### Treatment Method

- **Electrostatic spraying:** EPA-registered solution applied with electrostatic technology that wraps around surfaces for complete coverage. Standard for most residential and commercial treatments. Dries in 10-15 minutes.
- **Thermal fogging:** Disinfectant converted to fog that penetrates into HVAC, wall cavities, and hard-to-reach areas.
- **Ozone generation:** High-concentration ozone gas eliminates pathogens, bacteria, and odors. Requires vacating the property for 2-4 hours.
- **UV-C disinfection:** Ultraviolet light kills pathogens on exposed surfaces. Limited to line-of-sight.

### Biohazard Level

Standard residential disinfection: lower cost. Biohazard events (sewage backup, trauma, hoarding situations) require full PPE and specialized disposal: $1,000-$10,000+ depending on scope.

## When Do You Need Professional Sanitation in Madison?

### Post-Illness

After a household illness involving norovirus, influenza, or C. difficile, standard surface cleaning with household products is insufficient. EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants at proper concentration are required.

### Sewage Backup

Sewage contains fecal coliform, E. coli, hepatitis A, and other serious pathogens. After a sewage backup in a Madison home, professional sanitation is a health and safety necessity -- and typically required for insurance compliance.

### After Flooding

Floodwater contains soil, bacteria, and contaminants. After water damage restoration drying, professional sanitation of affected surfaces is recommended before rebuilding.

### HVAC Systems

Mold, bacteria, and allergens accumulate in HVAC ductwork over time. HVAC sanitation fogging introduces an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent into the system, reducing pathogen and allergen loads throughout the home.

### Real Estate Preparation

Professional sanitation before listing a home in Madison is increasingly common -- particularly after pets, illness, or long-term occupancy. It eliminates odors and provides documented treatment for buyers.

### Recurring Commercial Treatment

Schools, daycares, medical offices, gyms, and restaurants in Madison benefit from recurring professional disinfection. Monthly or bi-monthly treatments maintain baseline pathogen control.
