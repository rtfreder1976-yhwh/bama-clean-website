## Levels of Hardwood Floor Service

### Level 1: Basic Cleaning

Dry dust mopping, professional pH-neutral solution, and buff-dry. Removes surface dirt and light grime. Good for maintenance every 6-12 months.

### Level 2: Deep Cleaning

Professional cleaning solution, mechanical scrubbing with a low-RPM buffer, and extraction of loosened soil. Removes embedded grime and residue from consumer cleaning products.

### Level 3: Buff-and-Coat (Screen-and-Recoat)

Light abrasion of the existing finish, followed by application of a new layer of polyurethane. Does NOT sand down to bare wood. Adds a fresh protective layer, fills minor surface scratches, and restores shine. Best for floors that have lost gloss but have no deep scratches.

**Cost:** $1.50-$3.00/sqft. Result lasts 3-5 years.

### Level 4: Full Sand and Refinish

Sanding down to bare wood, staining (optional), and applying multiple coats of new finish. Removes deep scratches, gouges, and stains in the wood itself.

**Cost:** $3.00-$6.00/sqft. Result lasts 10-15 years.

## Huntsville Hardwood Floor Considerations

### Red Clay and Pollen

Huntsville has two major floor-damage culprits: red clay soil tracked in from outside and heavy spring pollen. Clay contains abrasive particles that scratch hardwood finish. Regular professional cleaning removes these abrasives before they damage the finish.

### Historic Twickenham Homes

Many Huntsville homes in the Twickenham Historic District and Five Points area have original hardwood floors from the early to mid-1900s. These may have wax or shellac finishes rather than modern polyurethane, requiring specialized cleaning methods. A professional identifies finish type before starting.

### Humidity

Huntsville humidity causes hardwood to expand and contract seasonally. Excess moisture from over-wet cleaning accelerates this. Professional low-moisture cleaning avoids water intrusion into seams.

### Steam Mops

Steam mops are marketed as safe for hardwood but are not. Steam forces moisture into wood seams, causing swelling, warping, and finish delamination. Multiple manufacturers void warranties when steam mops are used.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does hardwood floor cleaning take?**
Most Huntsville homes take 1-3 hours for basic or deep cleaning. Buff-and-coat takes 3-4 hours plus dry time.

**How long until I can walk on the floors?**
Basic cleaning: 30-60 minutes. Buff-and-coat: 4-8 hours minimum.

**Can pet stains be removed from hardwood?**
Surface stains respond to enzyme cleaning. Stains that have penetrated the wood require sanding.
