## Why Athens Has Elevated Mold Risk

Limestone County's geology is the root cause. Limestone is porous — it moves water. Homes built on limestone substrate, or with crawlspaces above limestone-rich soil, see more moisture migration than homes in other geological zones.

Add Limestone County's humidity — summer relative humidity regularly exceeds 75% outdoors and can reach 85%+ in an unencapsulated crawlspace — and you have ideal mold conditions. Mold needs moisture, organic material, and minimal airflow. Crawlspaces deliver all three.

Athens' older housing stock compounds this. Homes built before 1990 often have vented crawlspaces with bare dirt floors — the worst possible setup for moisture control. These homes are mold-inspection candidates every 2–3 years without exception.

Newer MTMUS-area subdivisions are better built, but slab construction can still develop mold in HVAC systems, bathroom caulking, and attic spaces if ventilation is inadequate.

- Before purchasing a home (especially older homes near downtown Athens)
- After any water damage event, if not already tested
