## What Drives Mold Inspection and Remediation Costs?

**Inspection scope:** Visual inspection alone identifies visible mold and moisture sources. Air sampling collects airborne spores for lab analysis -- this tells you the mold type and spore concentration compared to outdoor baseline.

**Material type:** Mold on non-porous surfaces (tile, glass, metal) can be cleaned. Mold in porous materials (drywall, insulation, wood framing) must typically be removed and replaced.

**Containment:** Professional remediation requires poly barriers, negative air pressure, and air scrubbers to prevent spore spread. These add $500-$2,000 to the job cost.

## Huntsville-Specific Mold Risks

### Older Housing Stock

Huntsville has significant older housing from the 1950s-1980s Space Race era. These homes may have poor ventilation, older vapor barriers (or none), and construction gaps that allow moisture intrusion. Wall cavity mold in older Huntsville homes is more common than in newer construction.

### Crawl Space Foundations

Many older Huntsville homes have crawl space foundations. North Alabama crawl spaces accumulate moisture from ground evaporation and can develop mold on structural framing. Encapsulation and remediation costs $2,000-$8,000.

### HVAC Systems

Older HVAC systems and improperly sized units can accumulate condensation in ductwork. Mold in Huntsville HVAC systems distributes spores throughout the home every time the system runs.

## When Do You Need Testing vs. Remediation?

**Need testing if:**
- You smell musty odor but cannot see mold
- Someone has unexplained respiratory symptoms
- You had water damage and are not sure if it dried completely
- You are buying or selling a home

**Skip straight to remediation if:**
- You can see visible mold covering more than 10 square feet
- Mold is visible in crawl space or attic
