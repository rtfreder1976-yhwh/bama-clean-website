## What Professional Sanitation Includes

Consumer disinfectant sprays cover surfaces you can see and reach. Professional sanitation uses electrostatic sprayers that wrap around objects, coat undersides of surfaces, and reach into crevices that a hand sprayer cannot. EPA-registered disinfectants at correct dwell times are applied, not wiped off prematurely.

## When Athens Homeowners Need Professional Sanitation

**After illness.** A household where someone has had influenza, norovirus, C. diff, or COVID may benefit from professional sanitation of high-touch surfaces, bathrooms, and common areas — particularly if immunocompromised individuals are present.

**After water or sewage damage.** Any Category 2 or Category 3 water event (gray water or black water) requires antimicrobial treatment as part of the restoration process. This is included in BamaClean's water damage restoration scope.

**After a biohazard event.** Unattended death, trauma, hoarding cleanup — these require professional sanitation and disposal protocols that go beyond residential sanitation.

**Scheduled commercial maintenance.** MTMUS-area businesses, schools, medical offices, and food service facilities in Athens benefit from scheduled professional sanitation as a complement to routine cleaning.
