## What Drives Mold Inspection Costs?

### Scope of the Inspection

A visual inspection identifies visible mold and moisture sources. Air sampling collects airborne mold spores for lab analysis -- this tells you the type of mold and spore concentration compared to outdoor baseline levels.

For suspected hidden mold (behind walls, under flooring), thermal imaging or moisture mapping may be added at $100-$300 additional.

### What Drives Mold Remediation Costs?

**Size of Affected Area:** Mold under 10 square feet can often be handled without professional remediation per EPA guidelines. Over 10 square feet, professional remediation is recommended. Over 100 square feet or any structural involvement: always professional.

**Material Type:** Mold on non-porous surfaces (tile, glass, metal) can be cleaned. Mold in porous materials (drywall, insulation, wood framing, carpet) must typically be removed and replaced.

**Containment Requirements:** Professional remediation requires containment -- poly barriers, negative air pressure, air scrubbers. These add $500-$2,000 to the job cost.

## Madison-Specific Mold Risks

### Humidity

Madison summer relative humidity regularly exceeds 80%. Any building material with elevated moisture content can grow mold within days. This is faster than most other regions.

### New Construction

Some newer Madison subdivisions have experienced moisture issues from improper vapor barrier installation, insufficient HVAC sizing, or windows without proper flashing. These lead to wall cavity moisture and mold that is not visible from inside.

### Crawl Spaces

Many older Madison homes have crawl space foundations. Crawl spaces in North Alabama accumulate moisture from ground evaporation and can develop mold on structural framing. Encapsulation and remediation costs $2,000-$8,000.

## When Do You Need Testing vs. Remediation?

**Need inspection/testing if:**
- You smell musty odor but cannot see mold
- Someone has unexplained respiratory symptoms
- You had water damage and are not sure if it dried completely
- You are buying or selling a home

**Skip straight to remediation if:**
- You can see visible mold covering more than 10 square feet
- Mold is visible in crawl space or attic

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does mold remediation take?**
Small areas (under 10 sqft): 1 day. Medium areas: 2-3 days. Large or structural: 1-2 weeks.

**Do I have to leave my home during remediation?**
For small contained areas, usually not. For major remediation, temporary relocation may be recommended.

**Will my insurance cover mold remediation?**
If mold resulted from a covered peril (burst pipe, roof damage), remediation is often covered. Mold from gradual leaks or maintenance neglect is typically excluded.
