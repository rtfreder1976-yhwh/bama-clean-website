## What Factors Affect Carpet Cleaning Prices in Madison?

### Square Footage

Most companies price by room or by square foot. Rooms over 250 sq ft often get charged as two rooms. Open floor plans can cost more than a house with the same footage divided into smaller bedrooms.

### Soil Level and Condition

Light soil: standard pricing. Heavy soil, tracked-in Alabama red clay, or matted high-traffic areas often add 20-30%. If your carpet has not been cleaned in 3+ years, expect to pay more.

### Pet Stains and Odor

Pet urine soaks through carpet into the pad. Surface cleaning will not fix it. Enzyme treatment runs $30-$80 extra per area. Full pet deodorization for a whole house: $100-$200 additional.

### Stairs

Most companies charge $3-$6 per step. A full staircase of 14 steps adds $40-$85.

## What Carpet Cleaning Methods Are Available?

### Hot Water Extraction (Steam Cleaning)

The industry standard recommended by every major carpet manufacturer. Injects hot water and solution into the carpet pile, then extracts it with powerful suction. Drying time: 4-8 hours. This is what BamaClean uses — truck-mounted equipment, not portable units.

### Dry/Low-Moisture Cleaning

Uses chemical compounds or minimal water. Faster drying but does not reach deep soil.

### Encapsulation

A polymer solution traps soil crystals that are then vacuumed up. Good for commercial light-soil maintenance, not ideal for family homes.

## How Often Should Madison Homeowners Clean Their Carpets?

North Alabama is tough on carpet:

- **Spring pollen** — among the worst pollen counts in the country
- **Alabama clay** — red and orange soil that builds up in carpet fibers
- **Summer humidity** — moisture that encourages mold growth
- **Fall mud** — kids tracking in from youth sports at Madison City schools

**General schedule:**
- No pets, no kids: every 18 months
- Pets or kids: every 12 months
- Pets AND kids: every 6-9 months
- After any flooding: immediately

## Red Flags When Hiring Carpet Cleaners in Madison

**$29.95/room advertised specials.** These are bait-and-switch. The upsells begin the moment they arrive.

**No truck-mounted equipment.** Portable machines work for apartments. A house needs truck-mounted extraction — more suction, hotter water, faster drying.

**Pressure to decide on the spot.** Legitimate companies quote before they start.

**No company address.** Check for an actual business address, not just a phone number.
