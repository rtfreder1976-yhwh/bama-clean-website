## What Affects Carpet Cleaning Prices in Decatur?

### Square Footage

Most companies price by room or by square foot. Rooms over 250 sq ft often get charged as two rooms.

### Soil Level

Light soil: standard pricing. Heavy soil, Alabama clay, or matted high-traffic areas often add 20-30%. Carpet not cleaned in 3+ years costs more -- it takes longer and more solution.

### Pet Stains and Odor

Pet urine soaks through carpet into the pad. Surface cleaning will not fix it. Enzyme treatment runs $30-$80 extra per area. Full pet deodorization: $100-$200 additional.

### Stairs

Most companies charge $3-$6 per step. A full staircase of 14 steps adds $40-$85.

## Carpet Cleaning Methods

### Hot Water Extraction (Steam Cleaning)

The industry standard. Injects hot water and solution into the carpet pile, then extracts with powerful suction. Drying time: 4-8 hours. BamaClean uses truck-mounted equipment -- stronger suction and hotter water than any portable machine.

### Dry/Low-Moisture Cleaning

Uses chemical compounds or minimal water. Faster drying but does not reach deep soil.

### Encapsulation

Polymer solution traps soil crystals that are then vacuumed up. Good for commercial maintenance. Not ideal as a primary method for family homes.

## How Often Should Decatur Homeowners Clean Their Carpets?

- No pets, no kids: every 18 months
- Pets or kids: every 12 months
- Pets AND kids: every 6-9 months
- After any flooding or water intrusion: immediately

Decatur-specific factors that accelerate soil buildup:
- **Tennessee River humidity** -- moisture that keeps carpet damp longer
- **Red clay soil** -- abrasive particles that damage carpet fibers when walked on
- **Industrial area dust** -- Decatur has significant manufacturing on the east side of town
- **Heavy pollen season** -- March through May from deciduous trees along the river
