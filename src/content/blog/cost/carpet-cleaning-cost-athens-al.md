## What Drives Carpet Cleaning Cost Up or Down?

**Room size.** A 12×14 bedroom and a 20×22 great room are both "one room" — but they're not the same job. Reputable cleaners price by square footage or actual room size, not a flat per-room rate.

**Soil and traffic level.** Athens has grown fast around the MTMUS (Mazda Toyota Manufacturing) plant. Newer homes off Hwy 72 and US-31 have young families with kids, dogs, and heavy foot traffic through main living areas. Heavily soiled carpet takes more passes, more solution, and more time.

**Pet damage.** Urine crystals bond to carpet fibers at a molecular level. Standard hot-water extraction doesn't fully break them down. A proper enzyme pre-treatment breaks the bond before extraction, then a deodorizing rinse follows. Skip this step and the smell comes back — especially in Limestone County's humid summers.

**Carpet fiber type.** Wool, Berber, and frieze carpets require lower-moisture methods. Historic Athens homes near downtown sometimes have original wool carpeting that needs specialist handling.

**Stairs and accessibility.** Stairs cost more — they're labor-intensive and require specialized tools. Multi-story homes take longer to set up and move equipment through.

## Is Professional Carpet Cleaning Worth It in Athens?

Yes — and the math makes it obvious.

Renting a machine costs $40–$60. Those machines run at a fraction of the water pressure and heat of a truck-mounted professional unit. They leave more moisture in the carpet, which creates secondary mold risk (Limestone County humidity is real), and they don't extract deeply enough to remove embedded allergens.

For families near the MTMUS plant or in Athens' newer subdivisions, there's a real health case for deep cleaning. Carpet traps dust mites, pet dander, pollen, and mold spores. When the HVAC runs, those particles circulate. Regular professional cleaning — every 12–18 months — removes what vacuuming cannot reach.

Financially: carpet replacement in a 3-bedroom Athens home costs $3,500–$8,000. Professional cleaning every 18 months at $180–$220 per visit extends carpet life by years. That math is not close.

## How to Prepare (and What to Expect)

**Vacuum first.** It takes 10 minutes and removes loose surface soil so the cleaning solution works on embedded dirt instead.

**Move small furniture.** We move most furniture, but removing chairs, lamps, and end tables from the rooms being cleaned speeds things up.

**Plan for drying time.** Athens humidity from May through September extends drying from a standard 4–6 hours to 6–10 hours. Run your HVAC, ceiling fans, and dehumidifier after we leave.

**Secure pets.** Setup involves doors open during equipment staging. A secured dog or cat means a smoother job.
